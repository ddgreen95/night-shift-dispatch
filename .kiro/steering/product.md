# Product

Night-Shift Dispatch is a fictional night-shift console for Hollow Pines Home Repair, a home-service shop that exists only as sample data for the Kiroween 2026 Frankenstein demo.

The owner uses one screen to watch six sample agents, step through a canned overnight script, and turn a pasted on-site note into a short summary plus a client-safe draft.

## Who it is for

The owner rehearsing the contest demo, and judges looking at a browser console that joins a 3D lot, a 2D board, and a writing desk.

## What it must feel like

A quiet Halloween night shift: moon, porch light, pines, warm windows. Spooky and calm. No gore, no jump scares, and no outside brands or copyrighted characters.

## Sample roster

The only agents in the product are:

- Night Dispatcher, in the Lantern Tower
- Ticket Triage, in the Intake Cottage
- Parts Runner, in the Parts Shed
- Invoice Drafter, in the Ledger Loft
- Callback Keeper, in the Bell Porch
- Review Requests, at the Thank-You Post

Statuses are idle, working, and waiting for a human yes. Tickets in the log are fictional, such as the Maple Lane sample ticket, the Wren Cottage sample job, and the Cedar Court sample draft.

## Hard boundaries

- Sample data only. Do not add real customers, real people, or a second business.
- Do not add secrets, API keys, or filled `.env` files.
- The desk summarizes locally. A visitor key, if someone types one, stays in browser `localStorage`.
- This build does not send, post, or bill.
- Do not deploy the demo or change who can see the repository unless the owner asks.

## How the owner will continue

The spec lives in `/.kiro/specs/night-shift-dispatch/`. The owner finishes and iterates in Kiro. Optional tasks in `tasks.md` are still open on purpose.
